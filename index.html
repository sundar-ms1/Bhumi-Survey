Land Surveying FAQs

Common questions about Total Station, topographic, contour and land surveying services.

What is a Total Station survey?

A Total Station survey measures angles, distances, positions and elevations and is commonly used for construction layout, site measurement, boundary work and engineering surveys.

Do you provide land surveys in Bangalore?

Bhumi Survey provides land surveying, Total Station, topographic, contour, site level and boundary demarcation services in Bangalore and other project locations in Karnataka.

What is a topographic survey?

A topographic survey records ground elevations, contours, physical site features and other survey information used for planning, engineering and construction.
